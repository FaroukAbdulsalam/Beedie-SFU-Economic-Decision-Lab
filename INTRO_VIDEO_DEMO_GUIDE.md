# Introductory Video Demo Guide

The goal is not to explain every model. It is to create curiosity.

## Suggested 90-second sequence

### 1. Module 1
Show the baseline demand and supply equations. Move the demand-shift slider and show equilibrium changing. Then open **Elasticity & Pricing** and show the automatic interpretation and contribution-margin comparison.

Suggested line:
> During the course, we will learn why these curves move, what happens to equilibrium, and what that means for a manager.

### 2. Module 3
Open **AI Changes the Trade-off** and move one AI productivity slider.

Suggested line:
> Technology can change productivity, opportunity cost, and even where production should take place. The dashboard updates the decision, but our job in class is to understand the economics underneath it.

### 3. Module 4
Select **Currency depreciation or falling reserves** and show the policymaker objective, policy menu and NorthStar implication. Then open **Price Ceiling / Floor** and move the controlled price below equilibrium to create a shortage.

### 4. Module 5
Open **Experiment: Scale / Retest / Stop** and move treatment conversion until the recommendation changes.

Closing line:
> By the end of the course, you should not simply know what the dashboard says. You should understand why it says it, what assumptions sit underneath it, and what would change the recommendation.
