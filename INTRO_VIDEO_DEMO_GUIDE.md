# Introductory video demo guide (90 seconds)

The goal is curiosity, not coverage. Every tool ends with an answer and a ruler showing what would flip it.

1. **Course overview.** Show the five-step NorthStar chain across the top.
   > One company, five decisions. Each answer comes with the condition that would change it.
2. **Module 1 → Profit-maximizing price.** Raise the unit cost slider from 500 to 600 and watch the price move
   from 900 to 950. Open "Predict first" to show the habit we will practise.
3. **Module 2 → Repeated game.** Lower the chance the rivalry continues until the ruler crosses δ* = 0.38.
   > Stable prices need no agreement. They depend on how much the future counts.
4. **Module 3 → Delivered cost.** Set the tariff on Asian boards to 10% and watch the recommendation switch to Mexico.
5. **Module 4 → Exposure map.** Point at the bars: a recession costs far more than a weaker dollar.
6. **Module 5 → Uncertainty & scaling.** Move "people per arm" and watch the smallest detectable effect shrink.
   > By the end of the course you should know why the app says what it says, and what would change it.
